Mulembe

A calm, native Mac terminal for running Claude Code, Codex and other AI coding agents side by side. Panes arrange themselves in a grid, turn orange when an agent needs you, and keep running through crashes and restarts. Every pane can use its own Claude account and its own git branch.

macOS app and its website · 14 days free, then $19 once

Visit mulembe-app.github.io

49 - Focus Booster

A one-minute focus game for iPhone. Find the numbers 1 to 49 in order on a shuffled board, as fast as you can. Ranks, a new Daily Challenge every day, ghost races against friends, Game Center leaderboards and iCloud sync, in nine languages.

iPhone game and its website · Free, in open beta on TestFlight

Visit 49game.github.io

Dozelight

A tiny menu bar app that takes the screen warmer than Night Shift allows, down to candlelight and pure red. It follows your bedtime, or dusk and dawn, so the evening warms up on its own. On Mac and Windows, in English and Polish.

macOS and Windows app and its website · 14 days free, then $9.99 once

Visit dozelight.github.io

Bieg Niepodległości RUNPOLAND

The website of a 10 km run through the streets of Poznań on Polish Independence Day, 11 November. A custom WordPress theme with a race-day countdown and a trophy room for every medal since 2016, plus the page content, SEO, a PHP 8 upgrade and a full UX pass.

Website · WordPress

Visit biegniepodleglosci.pl

A slower end to the day

There is a moment after sunset when the light outside turns amber and everything seems to slow down. Screens, though, keep glowing with the same bright daylight white long after the sun has gone.

Warmer light in the evening is easier on tired eyes. Late at night, a screen that glows soft red lets you finish a chapter or answer one last message without the harsh brightness of midday.

Tomorrow’s plan: a short walk before dinner, the second half of the novel, and lights out by eleven.

One person, the whole way

Artur PerećApps and websites, built with AI agents

I make careful software, mostly for Apple’s platforms, and I take each product the whole way: product design, engineering, website, App Store listing, release notes and support.

For me, creativity matters most. With AI agents doing the heavy lifting, my time goes into the idea itself: what to make, how it should feel, and the small details that make people want to come back to it.

I started as a programmer, with games in Unity and VR, then Java, C# and Swift. Today I lead a team of AI coding agents: I set the product direction, write the specifications, run several agents in parallel and review every change before it ships. That’s how one person keeps a Mac app, an iPhone game, a Windows port and four websites moving at once.

Every app here began as something I wanted for myself. I run several AI coding agents at once, so I built a terminal for exactly that. Night Shift wasn’t warm enough at midnight, so Dozelight goes further. A minute of focus training is easier to keep up than twenty, so a game of 49 takes about a minute.

I run too, which is how I came to rebuild a race’s website. If you have a product that should feel this calm, I’d be glad to hear about it.
